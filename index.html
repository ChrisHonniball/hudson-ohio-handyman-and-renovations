Hi, I'm Chris, a business owner based right here in Hudson, Ohio. I started this company to help homeowners like you take care of repairs, maintenance, and renovations with quality craftsmanship and dependable service.

I offer a wide range of services—including drywall repair, painting, trim work, custom woodworking, minor plumbing and electrical, and full room renovations. Whether it's a small fix or a bigger project, I approach every job with care, precision, and a focus on getting it done right the first time.

Clients often tell me they appreciate my clear communication, fair pricing, and reliable scheduling. I'm fully insured and background-checked through The Home Depot Pro Referral program, so you can feel confident knowing who's working in your home.
