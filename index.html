Hi, I'm Chris, a local handyman, woodworker, and resident in Hudson, Ohio. With a passion for fixing and creating things, I'm here to help you tackle all those household tasks you've been putting off. From repairing a leaky faucet, installing a security system, installing shelves, or creating a completely custom piece of decor or organization, I handle it all with precision and care.

Why spend your weekends trying to DIY when you can rely on me to get it done quickly and professionally? I take pride in offering reliable and hassle-free service, ensuring every job is done right the first time. No task is too small, and I'm always just a text, email, or phone call away.

I treat your home like my own, making sure everything is working perfectly so you can enjoy your space stress-free. Let me take care of your home's needs while you focus on what matters most.

Fast. Friendly. Reliable. Let me help you knock out your "to-do" list!
