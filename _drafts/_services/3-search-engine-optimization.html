Think of a phrase your customers would be typing into Google where you would like to be the first result.

Now... with that specific search phrase in mind. Think about the content that's online about your business. Do they match up?

By "match up" I don't mean, do you offer a product or service that fits that phrase. What I'm talking about is, does your company show a potential customer that you are a leader in the space AND does your online presence (website content, social media posts, etc) target that niche?

Getting the customer to see you is only 25% of the battle. What good is popping up in your prospect's online feed if the user and doesn't pay any attention to you?

The way to prevent these missed opportunities is to create a multi-faceted content strategy that presents your business as an authority in your niche.

Again let's think of the search phrase you picked. Let's imagine that your website SEO got the prospect to you but now...

...they see blog articles about the subject

...YouTube videos from you covering the topic

...and, when they visit your social media, they see that you are talking about that very topic with other followers.

Would that paint the picture that your company is the go-to place for what they seek?

This type of content plan not only solidifies you in the mind of your customer but also your position within your industry.
