Customers are able to identify advertising quicker than ever. This makes it harder-and-harder for your advertising to gain the attention it deserves.

The problem is... most businesses are advertising selfishly. They are creating content that benefits their business (which I know, sounds logical and correct. We'll get to that later). They are pushing "look at me" content rather than showing how they can help their customer.

These businesses are thinking about the product (or service) that they offer and presenting the raw facts of what their offer does. It's an honest attempt to get people to buy but it doesn't hit any of the key emotional or rational motivations that help people make a choice to hit "order now."

It's important that you take the time to understand the underlying buying motives that can influence your interactions with prospects.
— HubSpot

As a business it's key for you (with my help) to position your content strategy so that your creating posts and ads for your customer that show them the value of what you're offering. When we put in the extra work creating advertising that truly connects with your customer you'll get more likes, shares, and those coveted referrals that you're looking for.

To really dive in deep about how big brands achieve this, check out my blog series on buyer motivation.

We'll use discovery exercises that identify your unique selling point and how it positively influences your customers' lives. The content that we produce will inspire intrigue and generate curiosity so that your promos don't get passed by. We'll eliminate the "look at me" style of advertising from your marketing strategy and focus on providing real value to your followers so that your brand is looked at as an niche leader.
